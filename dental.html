Invisalign® clear aligners

Straighter teeth, without the wires.

Invisalign® uses a series of clear, custom-made aligners that gradually move your teeth. They are removable, so you can take them out to eat and brush, and they are much less noticeable than braces. We scan your teeth, show you the planned tooth movement, and give you an estimated treatment time before you decide.

May suit

  • Mild to moderate crowding
  • Small gaps between teeth
  • Teeth that moved after braces
  • More complex bite problems may be referred to an orthodontist

Dental implants

A fixed way to replace missing teeth.

A dental implant is a small titanium post that replaces the root of a tooth, with a custom crown on top to help restore chewing and appearance. At High on Smile, implants are planned and placed by Dr. Melwin, an MDS oral & maxillofacial surgeon. Whether an implant is right for you depends on your bone, gums and general health, which we assess first.

What we offer

  • Single tooth implants
  • Multiple teeth and implant bridges
  • Full-mouth implants (All-on-4 / All-on-6)
  • Immediate implants, where suitable
  • Bone grafting and sinus lift for low bone

Smile design

A smile designed for your face.

Smile design is a planned makeover. We look at the shape, colour, size and line of your teeth and gums alongside your face and lips, then discuss changes that aim to look natural on you. Depending on what you need, your plan may combine whitening, bonding, veneers, crowns, gum contouring or aligners.

Can include

  • Digital smile preview before treatment
  • Teeth whitening
  • Veneers and laminates
  • Composite bonding
  • Gum contouring
  • Crowns and bridges
