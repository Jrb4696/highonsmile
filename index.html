Do dental implants hurt?

Implant placement is done under local anaesthetic. Some soreness and swelling afterwards is common and is usually managed with simple pain relief for a few days. We give you clear aftercare instructions and check on you at a review visit.

How long do dental implants last?

With good oral hygiene, regular reviews and no smoking, implants can last many years. Longevity depends on your bone, gum health and how well you look after the surrounding teeth. The crown on top may need replacing sooner than the implant itself.

Am I suitable for implants if I have lost bone?

Often, yes. Low bone can sometimes be addressed with bone grafting or a sinus lift. We assess your bone, gums and general health first and explain what is realistic in your case.

How long does Invisalign treatment take?

It depends on how much movement is needed. Many mild to moderate cases take between 6 and 18 months, wearing aligners around 20 to 22 hours a day. After your scan we show you the planned movement and an estimate before you commit.

Will smile design look natural?

The aim is a result that suits your face rather than a generic “perfect” smile. We consider the shape, shade and line of your teeth and gums alongside your face and lips, and show you a preview before treatment where possible.

Are aesthetic treatments safe?

Injectable and energy-based treatments carry risks and are not suitable for everyone. Every treatment starts with a consultation where suitability, benefits, limitations, risks and aftercare are discussed, and nothing proceeds without your informed consent.

Do you offer EMI or card payments?

Please ask at your consultation. We will talk through payment options and give you a written estimate before treatment starts, so there are no surprises.

What are your clinic timings?

Monday to Saturday, 9:30 AM to 1 PM and 5 to 9 PM. Sunday, 10 AM to 1 PM. Evening slots are popular, so it is worth booking a little ahead.
